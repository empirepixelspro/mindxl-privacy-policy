Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MindXL (developed by Empire Pixels Pro) handles information when you use our Android application. MindXL has no backend server — your schedule, notes, categories, reminders, medication and parking records stay on your own device. There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud sync. By using the app, you agree to the use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Target Audience & Age Appropriateness

MindXL is a general-audience productivity app intended for users aged 13 and above — students, professionals, shift workers and families planning their daily schedule. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app contains no social features, chat, user-generated public content or sign-in.

2. Geographical Distribution

This application is distributed globally through the Google Play Store, with the explicit exception of Israel (IL). The service is not licensed or supported for use within this region.

3. Information the App Accesses

The app accesses the following only on your device, and only after you grant the permission where one is required:

  • Calendar events, notes, tasks, categories, reminders, medicine schedules, parking spots and settings: created and stored by you in a local database on your device. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.
  • Location (optional): requested only when you tap “Use Current Location” inside Map Note or Parking, to centre the OpenStreetMap view and save a parking position. Location is used on the device only and is not uploaded.
  • Camera and photos (optional): requested only when you attach a photo to a parking note or pick a custom category icon. Images stay on your device.
  • Device calendar (optional): read only when you explicitly import events from your device calendar. Nothing is written back without your action.
  • Notifications and alarms: used locally to deliver the reminders you create. No notification content leaves your device.
  • Files (optional): used only when you export or import a JSON/CSV backup to a folder you choose.

4. Backup, Export and Data Deletion

All backups are plain files written to a location you select on your own device or storage; we never receive a copy. You can erase everything instantly from the app's Settings → Data & Backup → delete/reset option, or by uninstalling the app. Because there is no account on our side, there is no server record to delete.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of MindXL shows a small banner advertisement supplied by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and app interaction data. This collection is performed by Google under its own privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Ads are shown only to users without an active subscription. Subscribers never see ads, and the advertising SDK is not used on the paid experience. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

6. Subscriptions & Payments

MindXL Pro is sold as an auto-renewing subscription through Google Play Billing. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your card number. The app only receives the purchase state (active or not) and the price shown to you by Google Play. Manage or cancel any time in Google Play → Subscriptions.

7. Maps & Third Parties

Map Note uses OpenStreetMap map tiles (OSMdroid) and the device's built-in geocoder. When map tiles load, your IP address and the requested tile coordinates are sent to the OpenStreetMap tile servers under the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy (https://wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). Address lookups may use your device's geocoding service provider. The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no advertising networks other than AdMob.

8. Data Security

Your data is stored in app-private storage protected by the Android sandbox. Network requests made by the ad, billing and map SDKs use HTTPS. Because nothing is stored on a server, there is no online database of yours to breach.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date. For any privacy question or a data-deletion request, contact us:
